Behind it all

The team at The Taniwha Times is a dedicated group of east students who gather together to discuss themes, articles and ideas to put together for each issue. Once the theme is decided, the work for each issue is divided and each member is assigned an article or two, and then everone gets to work interviewing, reviewing, and creating the articles you find in your very own Taniwha Times news paper issue. More work than you'd imagine is put into each issue, and our team is sure to put lots of love into our issues to, only preaching truth and honesty. We are very grateful to everyone who reads and supports our newsletter.

Lots of love - The team at Taniwha Times xoxo

From the top

Taniwha Times newspaper was founded in 2023 by East student Lucy Thorburn. And many of the articles from that first edition are still hits in our newer editions too, like the Tara Tells section. Did you know that one of the earliest pieces of Tara's advice was "What do I do if I've dropped my vape in the toilet?" Unfortunately, this landed the paper in some pretty deep trouble, but nonetheless the Taniwha Times continues today!

The papers are generally published every three weeks, but can have some variation depending on content and holidays. The papers are normally distributed in the morning at the front entrance of the school, or during lunchtime around the place. Don't worry if you miss an issue though! You can usually find them in the library on librarians desk :)
